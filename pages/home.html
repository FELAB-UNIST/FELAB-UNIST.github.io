Research & Writing Guide to Students

A bilingual field guide for student researchers, distilled from recurring feedback on more than 80 research papers, presentations, and reports, together with over 40 OpenReview reviews written by Prof. Yongjae Lee. GPT was used to identify recurring themes and organize them into ten practical principles for framing research questions, aligning claims with evidence, designing reliable experiments, and communicating results clearly.
